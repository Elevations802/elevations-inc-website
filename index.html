FRACTIONAL FINANCE FAQ

The financial support your business needs, without the full-time overhead.

What does a fractional controller do?

A fractional controller provides part-time, controller-level leadership for accounting operations, monthly close, reporting, reconciliations, internal controls, and management insight—without the cost of a full-time hire.

When does a business need fractional CFO support?

Businesses often benefit from fractional CFO support when they need stronger cash-flow planning, forecasts, financial modeling, KPI reporting, or decision support during growth, change, or a major project.

Can Elevations support multi-entity businesses?

Yes. Elevations provides multi-entity accounting, consolidated reporting, holding-company structure support, CAPEX oversight, and financial management for complex operations.
